# Report page structure (one notebook or script)

Defaults for a report page.

- Research is fluid: reshape or drop the parts that do not help the reader.
- The principles are firmer than the outline.

## Principles

1. **Question first.** The heading of a step is usually its question.
   - Validation and descriptive EDA steps can have a descriptive heading.

2. **Context just in time.** Methods, math and data subsets go in the step that needs them.
   - This can repeat details. That is fine.
   - Up front: only the data overview and shared definitions.

3. **Order by the argument,** not always by the notebook.
   - Usually: setup and validation, main results, then explanations.

4. **Show everything, clearly.**
   - Every figure and table of the notebook is on the page.
   - Tangential or redundant ones go in the appendix.
   - Skip only what Aviv says to skip, and WIP results.
   - Numbers go in tables, not only in prose.

5. **Facts first.**
   - Observations and titles of results state only what the shown figures and tables support.
   - Exact numbers go in the body, not in titles.
   - Add a conclusion or an interpretation only after Aviv states it.
   - Mark a hypothesis as a hypothesis.

6. **Document unexpected findings.** They feed future work.

7. **Methods are complete and exact** (`research-methods`).
   - A reader can redo the analysis from the page alone.
   - A reader can find where a result could be wrong.

## Outline

1. **Provenance** (gray, first line):
   - "Generated by Claude from `<notebook or script>` at `<repo>@<sha>`, ..."
   - List each repo the analysis uses. Add "+ local changes" if the tree has changes.
   - Then: "Runs: `<ids>`. Last update `<date>`."
   - A link back to `index.html`.

2. **Questions** this notebook addresses, one line each, as in the index.

3. **Table of contents** (links to step anchors).

4. **Data overview:** runs, cohorts and splits.
   - A table of counts (subjects, sessions) per cohort and split.
   - Short: the exact data and filters of each step are in its Methods.

5. **Shared model or definitions** (only if several steps use them).
   - A model that predicts what each figure should show is the strongest frame.
   - Later steps confirm or break it.

6. **Steps,** ordered by the argument (see "Step block").
   - Group related steps under one heading with a sub-question when it helps.

7. **Other findings:** unexpected observations, with links to their steps.

8. **Open questions and next checks,** ordered by value.
   - WIP results are listed as pending.

9. **Appendix** (collapsed):
   - metric definitions;
   - per-session figures;
   - items left out of the main flow;
   - reproducibility: notebook, commits, run ids, data snapshot date, changelog.

Question-level parts are in the index (`index.md`): motivation, short answer, global conclusions, caveats.

## Step block

Core parts:

- **Heading:** number and question or check, e.g. `2. Does the gap shrink with lambda?`
  - No "Step" word.
  - A stable anchor (`id="s2"`) for links from the index and the hub.
  - Sub-steps: `2.1`, `2.2`.

- **Why:** 1-2 lines.
  - What this result probes, and how it helps answer the question.

- **How:** 1-2 sentences in simple words, with the one equation the reader needs.
  - Directly after it: the **Methods** toggle (see "Methods").

- **Method diagram(s)** (for a non-trivial method), directly after "How".
  - Follow `research-methods`.
  - Take it from `<repo_out_dir>/<analysis_name>/diagrams/`. If none exists, make it, save it there, and tell Aviv.
  - Presentations reuse it.

- **Figure(s) or table(s).**
  - Caption: one line on what it shows.
  - Directly below: **How to interpret** (axes, colors, what one point is, what "good" or "no effect" looks like).
  - For a table: what each column means, and the rule for bold values.

- **Observations:** facts only.
  - The few most important ones.
  - Numbers where they add to the figure or table.

Add when they apply:

- **Expectation** (before the figure): what we expect if the hypothesis is true, from the spec's predictions.
  - Usually not needed for a sanity check.
- **Conclusion** (local): only as agreed with Aviv. Else "Conclusion: not yet discussed".

Two related figures can share one "Why" and one "How".

## Methods

The Methods toggle of each step describes the method of that step.

- Write and check it with `research-methods` (load it first). Depth: *Full*.
- A long toggle is fine: use the toggle for length, not for less detail.

## HTML layout

- One complete HTML file per page (`<!doctype html>`, `<title>`, linked `assets/report.css`).
- Readable width. Sticky or top table of contents for long pages.
- Utilitarian: polished type and spacing, no hero, no decoration.

**Font size:**

- Base 18px on `html`.
- All other sizes in `rem` (body 1rem, captions and tables not below 0.86rem).

**Publish-ready by default** (the Artifact page contract, where it costs nothing):

- Colors as tokens on `:root`, with the two dark-mode blocks.
- Explicit `body` background, and a 16px side gutter.
- Wide tables in an `overflow-x: auto` container.
- A short `<title>` (e.g. "Headset metrics 020").
- Scripts only from cdnjs (or jsDelivr, unpkg), at a pinned version.
- The `artifact-design` skill has the full contract. Load it when you set up the page style or have a layout question.

**Zoom:** a click on a figure opens it in an overlay.

- Viewer.js: wheel or toolbar to zoom, drag to pan, click outside or Esc to close.
- Copy `template/report.css` and `template/report.js` to `reports/assets/`.
- Link both in each page (`<script defer src="assets/report.js">`).
- `report.js` also builds the tabs and loads Viewer.js (pinned, cdnjs).

**Components:**

- **Toggles** (`<details>`): Methods, long derivations, metric definitions, per-session figures, the appendix.
- **Tabs:** variants of one figure (per session, per array, train/test).
- **Side by side:** two figures to compare.
- **Callouts:** blue = questions or short answer, yellow = caveat, green = recommendation.
- **Figures:** `<figure>` with a relative `<img src>` and a `<figcaption>`.
- **Tables:** all key numbers, with a noise floor or null column when one exists.

**Math:**

- MathJax 3 with SVG output (`tex-svg.js` from cdnjs, pinned).
- `$...$` and `$$...$$` (enable `$` as an inline delimiter).
- Not KaTeX: a published Artifact blocks its CDN fonts.

**Interactive plots** (Plotly, D3): only where interaction helps.

- Their data goes inline or in a `<script src="data.js">` file (a page from disk cannot `fetch()` local files).
- Form and colors: `dataviz`.
- Titles, units, statistics, legends: `self-explanatory-figures`. It wins where they disagree.

**Diagrams:** inline SVG, with `artifact-diagramming`.

- Inline the SVG files of `<repo_out_dir>/<analysis_name>/diagrams/` (content rules: `research-methods`).
- Static PNG figures from analysis code follow `self-explanatory-figures`.

## Writing rules

- ASD-STE100: short sentences, simple words, active voice, bullets over prose.
- No em or en dashes. Keyboard characters only.
- Facts only by default. Conclusions and interpretations only after Aviv states them.
- Verb strength matches the evidence:
  - "reduces", not "removes";
  - "is consistent with", not "shows", when other explanations are open;
  - "no change beyond noise", not "no effect".
- Numbers have units, and the noise floor or null next to a difference.
  - Say "within noise" when it is.
- Code names of metrics and variables in `backticks`, defined where they first appear.
- One step per result. Other places cite it, they do not restate it.
- Describe a figure once ("How to interpret"), then give observations.
